Selected Publication

Wiring Beats Blending: What Transfers Between Transformer Sizes — and What Doesn't

Ravi Satya Durga Prasad Yenugula · 2026 · arXiv:2608.02829 [cs.LG, cs.CL, cs.AI]

Can a pretrained large model be converted into a smaller sibling instead of training every size from scratch? I characterize the 1.4B→410M conversion in the Pythia family end to end: representations align across sizes but parameters do not, and conversion value lives in initialization. Decomposing that init into two independent levers — least-squares compensation and variance-preserving rescale — yields a token-efficient method that, at matched budget, also beats structured pruning with distillation, the standard pipeline, and improves further when combined with it.
